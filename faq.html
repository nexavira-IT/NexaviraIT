Frequently asked questions

A little clarity before we begin.

Ask us directly →
What kinds of projects does Nexavira IT take on?

We work on business websites, WordPress sites, e-commerce, custom web applications, interface design and ongoing website support. The right fit depends on your goals and scope; explore our services or tell us about your project.

How does a project usually get started?

We begin with a conversation to understand your business, users, constraints and priorities. From there, we can agree on a useful scope and next steps before work begins.

How long will my project take?

Timing depends on the type of work, how much content or functionality is involved, and how quickly decisions and materials are available. We’ll discuss a realistic schedule once we understand the project.

How much does a website or application cost?

There isn’t one price that fits every project. Cost depends on requirements, design, content, integrations and ongoing needs. Share what you have in mind and we can discuss an appropriate scope.

Can you work with a business outside Bangladesh?

Yes. Nexavira IT is based in Bangladesh and is open to working with local and international clients. We can discuss communication, working hours and project coordination at the start.

Will I be able to update my website myself?

Where it makes sense, we can build a content management experience suited to your team and provide a handover. The right approach depends on the project and the type of updates you need to make.

Do you provide support after launch?

Post-launch support and maintenance can be discussed as part of your project. We’ll clarify what’s included, what falls outside the agreed scope and what support options are available.

What should I prepare before contacting you?

A short description of your business, what you’d like to improve, any important deadlines and links to examples you like are a useful start. You don’t need a finished brief to begin the conversation.
