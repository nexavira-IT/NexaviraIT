The concept

Make a specialist offer easier to understand.

This fictional brief imagines a small consultancy that needs a clear digital home for its services, approach and inquiry path.

STUDIO

01 / The challenge

Visitors need a quick sense of fit.

In this imagined scenario, the consultancy’s work is nuanced and its prospective clients need enough context to decide whether to start a conversation.

02 / The direction

Put the essentials in a calm, clear order.

The concept uses a concise homepage, scannable service descriptions, an explanation of the working approach and a direct inquiry route. The visual system uses restrained color and generous spacing to give the content room.

03 / Considerations

Make the site manageable over time.

A production brief would confirm the real brand, content, accessibility requirements, analytics needs and editing workflow before choosing a platform or claiming outcomes.
